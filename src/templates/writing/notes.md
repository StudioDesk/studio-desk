# Notes

## The garden

- Maple Street Community Garden: 24 family plots, 6 shared beds, a tool shed and a rain barrel by the gate.
- The shared beds grow vegetables for the Eastside Food Pantry. Last season the garden gave the pantry 1,140 pounds of produce.
- Entrance on Maple Street, next to the library. Parking on the street; bike rack by the shed.

## This season's dates

- Orientation for new volunteers: Saturday, April 11, 9:00 to 10:00 a.m., at the tool shed.
- Season opens: Saturday, April 18.
- Volunteer days: every Saturday, 9:00 a.m. to noon, April 18 to October 31.
- Spring planting day (all hands): Saturday, May 2.
- Harvest celebration and potluck: Saturday, September 26, 4:00 to 7:00 p.m.

## What volunteers do

- Weeding, watering and mulching the shared beds.
- Harvesting on Saturdays and packing the pantry crates.
- Turning the compost (optional, gloves provided).

## What to bring

- Water bottle, sun hat, sturdy closed shoes.
- Gloves if you have them; the shed has spares.
- Nothing else: tools are in the shed.

## Who to ask

- Volunteer coordinator: Dana Okafor, at the garden every Saturday. Email: volunteers@maplestreetgarden.example
- On the day, look for the green apron.

## If you can't come

- Email the coordinator by Friday evening, or reply to the Thursday reminder email.
