# Brief: a welcome for new volunteers

**Who it's for:** people who have just signed up to volunteer at the Maple Street Community Garden, most of them for the first time.

**What we need:** writing that welcomes them, tells them what a volunteer day is like, and answers the questions new volunteers always ask, so nobody feels lost on their first Saturday.

**What it should cover:**

- What the garden is and who it feeds: the plots, the shared beds and the weekly harvest that goes to the Eastside Food Pantry.
- When volunteering happens, and the orientation every new volunteer comes to first.
- What to bring and what to wear.
- Who to ask for help on the day.
- How to let us know when they can't come.

**What it shouldn't do:** lecture about gardening, list rules in a wall of text, or promise anything the notes don't support.

The facts are in `notes.md`. How we sound is in `style.md`.
