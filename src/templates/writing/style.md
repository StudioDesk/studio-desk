# How we sound

- **Warm and plain.** Write the way a friendly neighbor talks: short sentences, everyday words, no jargon.
- **Speak to one person.** Use "you" and "we". Not "volunteers shall".
- **Lead with what matters.** Dates, times and places first; background after.
- **Be exact.** Use the dates, times and names in the notes as they are. Never invent a fact; if something is missing, leave it out or say who to ask.
- **Short is kind.** Headings and short lists where they help. No paragraph longer than four lines.
- **Sign off as the garden:** "See you in the garden! — Maple Street Community Garden".
